## Rules that apply to every picture

- **What they are:** real places in the students' world (campus gates, faculty buildings, lecture halls, libraries, a notice board), from Wikimedia Commons. None of them is AcadeGrade's office, users or rooms, and no caption may suggest it. The two 1962 photographs are archive pictures from the African Studies Centre Leiden and are always captioned with their date.
- **Treatment:** every file is greyscale with the slight indigo cast from `01` §4, baked in at export (greyscale, contrast 1.05, shadows pulled toward the ink `#18153D`, highlights lifted slightly toward the ground `#E7E5EE`). Pages use the files as they are, with no CSS filter. Untreated colour versions are not published.
- **Share-alike:** the CC BY-SA pictures are altered (cropped, resized, recoloured). The altered versions are shared under the same licence as the original, CC BY-SA 4.0. Every credit line for them ends "tinted, shared under the same licence" (shorter footer form: "tinted"), and the licence links to <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>.
- **CC0:** the Institute of African Studies picture needs no credit; it is credited anyway.
- **Footer form:** `formatCredit` in `credits.ts` prints "<title>, by <author> (CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons, tinted)" from each entry. The `creditLine` field holds the long form, ending "tinted, shared under the same licence", for figure captions and any credits page.

## ESUT (owner's university)

The owner asked for Enugu State University of Science and Technology (ESUT, Agbani) to be preferred for the home hero frame 1, the footer stamp and the About page. Searches on 17 Nov 2026 ("Enugu State University of Science and Technology", "ESUT Agbani", "ESUT campus", "Enugu State University", "ESUT", plus a direct Wikimedia Commons search and category check) found **no honest, openly licensed photograph of ESUT**:

- "Enugu state university gate" and "Enugu state university" (MediaMOF, CC BY-SA 4.0) are mislabelled. The sign in both reads "University of Nigeria, Enugu Campus" (UNEC), so they cannot be used as ESUT.
- "Departmental picture at Pharmacy Dept of ESUT University" is a group portrait of identifiable people, so it is ruled out.
- Everything else is unrelated (ESUT is also an airport code in Sweden) or shows Agbani roads and farmland, not the campus.

**No slot uses ESUT today.** Hero frame 1, the footer stamp and About use the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (also in Enugu State). If the owner takes or licenses an ESUT photograph (CC BY-SA or the owner's own), it can replace `unn-main-gate` by adding a new slug with the same crops (`4x5`, `16x9`, `stamp-3x4`) and changing the ids that the pages import.
