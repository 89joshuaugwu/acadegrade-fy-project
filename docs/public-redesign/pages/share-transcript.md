# Page plan · Shared transcript (`/share/[shareId]`)

A restyle. The fetch, the PIN rules, the states, the print and the PDF in `app/(public)/share/[shareId]/` and `lib/share/` stay exactly as they are; only the look, the copy, the layout and the states change. The shared record **is** a paper document, so this page is the site's best example of the stationery idea: the record is printed on the same slip the home page demonstrates. It inherits `01`–`04`, follows the structure of `pages/home.md`, and defines the **MinimalShell** that the other system pages reuse (§8.1).

## 1. Purpose

- **Mode:** System/Read. **Shell:** Minimal.
- **Visitor:** someone a student sent a link to: a parent, a sponsor, a scholarship or admissions officer, an employer, a classmate. Often on a phone from WhatsApp, sometimes on a desktop ready to print. Most have never heard of AcadeGrade.
- **First question:** "What is this record, and can I trust it?" Answer at the top of the paper: whose record it is, that it is self-reported and unofficial, which university the student entered, and until when the link works.
- **One action:** **Download PDF**. Print is a text link. The visitor next step ("What is AcadeGrade?") is a quiet text link below the paper.

## 2. The argument (each block exists because of one claim)

| # | Claim | Proof on the page | Block |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | This is a tidy, readable academic record | An A4 paper slip with the stripe, the header and real tables | The paper |
| 2 | It is honest about what it is | "Self-reported by the student. Not issued or verified by any university." as an ink band, and again on every page foot | Notice band, paper foot |
| 3 | The numbers are explained | Each semester's units, points, GPA and PI; the cumulative CGPA, PI, units and class | Semester tables, cumulative bar |
| 4 | It can leave the screen | Download PDF and Print that match the paper | Action row |
| 5 | The student controls it | The link's end date; the PIN screen; summary-only links | Action row, PIN state, summary table |
| 6 | You can find out what made it, if you want | One stub under the paper | Visitor stub |

## 3. First screen

### Desktop (1440×900)

- **Ground:** `--pg-ground` (light) or the night desk (dark). The paper stays paper in both.
- **MinimalShell header** (72px): mark and wordmark at left, linking home.
- **Action row** (print hidden), aligned to the paper's edges, 24px above it:
  - left, at small: "Shared with you through AcadeGrade · This link works until 30 November 2026";
  - right: **Download PDF** (primary, `size="sm"`, 44px) and the text link "Print".
- **The paper:** centred, 794px wide (210mm at 96dpi), at least 1123px tall (297mm) so it reads as an A4 sheet, square corners, no shadow, no border, padding 56px. The first screen shows the stripe, the header, the notice band, the student block and the start of the first semester table.

### Phone (390×844)

- Header 60px; the action row stacks: the line, then **Download PDF** at full width with "Print" beside it as a text link.
- The paper runs full width inside the 16px margins, padding 20px; the stripe 32px. First screen: stripe, header, notice band and the student block.

### Why this works

- The visitor sees a document, not an app. It looks like the slips students already know, set with care.
- The honesty line is the most prominent thing after the name, so nobody can mistake it for an official transcript.

## 5. Sections

### 5.1 Stripe and header (on the paper)

- Stripe band 40px (32px phone; 8mm in print), five flat blocks.
- Centred: the AcadeGrade mark (SVG, 32px; today a 10×10 `logo.png`), the h1 at lead, "Made with AcadeGrade" at small, then the university line at small.

### 5.2 Notice band

- A full-width ink bar inside the paper's padding, 48px, `SELF_REPORTED_NOTICE` in paper colour at body weight 500, centred, `role="note"` (kept; tested).
- Replaces today's red-bordered red box: red is for F grades and carry-overs only (`01` §4), and an ink band is more prominent, not less.

### 5.3 Student block

- A two-column definition list (`dl`) at body: Name, Matric number, Level ("200L"), then Department, Programme.
- **Passport photograph** (only when `showPhoto && avatarUrl`, carried): top-right of the block at 3:4, 30mm wide, square corners, in its own colours (it is the document's content, not site imagery, so no tint), alt "Photograph of {name}".
- No serif, no 2px black frame, no blur.

### 5.4 Semesters

- **Full links** (`shareLevel` absent or `'full'`): one real `<table>` per semester with a `<caption>` "100L First Semester · 2022/2023" at body weight 500 (today an `h3` in a grey band; keep an `h2` per semester for navigation and use the caption for the table's name). Rows at body size with tabular figures; 1px row rules inside the table only; numbers right-aligned.
  - **F grades:** the grade and point cells in `--pg-mark` (examiner red), the only red on the paper.
  - **Missing scores** print as a grey dash (carried `?? '-'`).
  - **Foot row** (`tfoot`, `--pg-paper-2`): "Semester total", units, points, GPA, PI (§4).
  - **Phone:** each row becomes two lines: line 1 the code and title (title wraps under the code), units, total, grade and points; line 2 at small in soft ink "CA 22 · Exam 52 · Point 5". Nothing is dropped; the page never scrolls sideways (today the table scrolls inside a 600px minimum width).
- **Summary links** (`'summary'`): the note (kept), then one table with Semester, Session, Units, GPA, PI and no course rows (carried; tested).
- **Malformed semesters** with no `courses` field render an empty table body with the foot row (carried tolerance; tested).
- **Page breaks** in print: `break-inside: avoid` per semester (carried).

### 5.5 Cumulative summary

- A perforation across the paper, then the cumulative block:
  - **ArcMeter** (small, 160px) at left: the five flat segments, the gold dot at the CGPA, `role="img"` with the label "CGPA 3.78 of 5.00, Second Class Honours (Upper Division)". It replaces the student-app `CGPAArc` with its "dramatic reveal" animation.
  - **Foot bar** at right (the Slip foot bar): an ink bar with paper text: "CGPA" with the figure on a gold cell in ink ("3.78 / 5.00"), then "PI 3.14 / 5.00", "Total units 27", and "Projected class" with `resolveDegreeClass(cgpa).transcriptLabel`.
- **Figures carried:** `resolveSharedStanding` (the server snapshot is authoritative; old snapshots recompute), `formatCgpa` / `roundCgpa` (half-up, as the dashboard; tested).

### 5.6 PIN state (`401 PIN_REQUIRED`, `PIN_INCORRECT`, `429 PIN_LOCKED`)

- A small `stub` slip centred on the ground, 440px (full width on phones): notch on its left edge, the mark, then:
  - h1 "This record needs a PIN" (tested `/needs a pin/i`);
  - sentence: "The student protected this link with a PIN. Ask them for it, then enter it here.";
  - field label "PIN" (tested `/^pin$/i`), `type="password"`, `inputMode="numeric"`, `autoComplete="off"`, 4–6 digits, tabular figures at lead with 0.2em tracking (no monospace, no cells: the length varies);
  - primary **View record** (busy "Checking…").
- **Carried rules:** no `name` and no `action` on the form, so the PIN can never reach a URL; the PIN travels only in the `X-Share-Pin` header; the field is cleared as soon as it is sent; focus returns to it after each answer.
- **Messages** (`role="alert"`, kept):
  - wrong format: "Enter the 4 to 6 digits the student gave you."
  - wrong PIN: "That PIN isn't right. Check it with the student and try again."
  - locked: "Too many wrong PINs. This link is locked for about 60 minutes. Try again later." (minutes from `retryAfterSeconds`, default 3600). The field and the button are disabled, and the line under the button says why: "Locked for now to protect the student's record."
- **Metadata stays generic** for PIN links (`shareMetadataFor`: no name in the title, description or preview).

### 5.7 Unavailable (`404` and `410`, shown the same way, carried)

- The same stub: h1 "This link has expired" (tested `/link expired/i`), sentence "Shared records stop opening after the date the student chose, or when they turn the link off. Ask them to send a new one. If you typed the address, check it." Primary **Go to AcadeGrade** (`/`).
- Expired and unknown links stay identical on purpose: an old link must not reveal whether a record ever existed.

### 5.8 Could not load (network or server error)

- Today this shows "Transcript Not Found", which is wrong (the record may be fine). New: h1 "We couldn't open this record", sentence "Check your connection and try again." Primary **Try again** (calls the same `load()`; no PIN is kept, so a PIN link asks again). Text link "Go to AcadeGrade".

### 5.9 Loading

- The paper shape at full size: the stripe, a grey bar for the h1, the notice band in `--pg-paper-2`, grey bars for the student block and for eight table rows at real row height. No shimmer, no layout shift when the record lands. A visually hidden status "Loading the record…".

### 5.10 Visitor stub (below the paper; print hidden; not in the PDF)

- 48px below the paper, a notched `stub` 560px wide (full width on phones) on the ground: "Made with AcadeGrade" at lead, the sentence at body, and the text link "What is AcadeGrade?" (§4). No button, no sign-up push, no account prompt. Shown only on the ready state.

### 5.11 Print and PDF

- **Print** (`window.print()`, carried): a print stylesheet replaces the `body * { visibility: hidden }` hack. `@page { size: A4; margin: 12mm }`; the shell, the action row and the visitor stub are `print:hidden`; the paper prints edge to edge of the margins. `print-color-adjust: exact` on the stripe. Because many browsers skip backgrounds when printing, ink bars (the notice band and the cumulative bar) switch in print to ink text between two 2px ink rules, so the words can never print white on white.
- **Download PDF** (`jspdf` + `jspdf-autotable`, carried content and order): header, student block with the optional photo, semester tables (or the summary table), cumulative summary, the notice on every page foot with "Page n of m" (tested), file name `{name}_Transcript.pdf`.
  - **Restyle:** the five-block stripe drawn as flat rectangles at the top of page 1; the h1 and meta as on the paper; the notice as an ink band; table heads in ink with paper text (today a blue `41,128,185` fill); F grades in examiner red; the "Points" column renamed; the cumulative block as an ink bar with the CGPA on a gold cell and a small five-segment arc with a gold dot (replacing the two grey-and-indigo gauges).
  - **Load on demand:** `jspdf` and `jspdf-autotable` move to a dynamic `import()` inside the click handler (today they are imported at the top, so every visitor downloads them). The button shows "Preparing PDF…" while it loads.
  - **Failure:** if generation throws, an inline line under the action row: "We couldn't make the PDF. Use Print and choose Save as PDF instead."

## 6. Imagery for this page

- **None besides the mark and the student's own photograph.** The paper is the picture. This is defended for the inspector's image floor: a System/Read page whose object is the document.
- **Open Graph image:** a generic stationery slip (stripe, "Shared academic record", "Not issued or verified by any university") generated with `next/og` at 1200×630, the same for every link. It never carries a name, a CGPA or a photo. Today it is `logo.png` declared as 800×600.

## 7. Motion on this page

- **Ready:** the short "laid down" move for the paper (rise 16px with opacity over 320ms, the stripe filling at 60ms a block). Once per page load.
- **ArcMeter:** the dot is placed at once (no reveal tween; the reader came to read, not to watch).
- **PIN stub:** rises 16px with opacity; a wrong PIN shakes the stub once (400ms, transform only).
- **Removed:** the scale-in on the PIN and error cards, the action-bar drop-in, the `animateOnMount` arc reveal.
- **Reduced motion:** everything appears at once.

### 8.1 MinimalShell (shared by share pages, maintenance, offline, iOS and 404)

- **Header:** 72px (60px phone) on `--pg-ground`: the mark (SVG, inline) and wordmark at the left margin, linking to `/` with the accessible name "AcadeGrade home". Nothing else.
- **Main:** `<main id="content">` with the page's object; a skip link "Skip to content" first.
- **Imprint line** at the bottom, small, soft ink, centred: "© 2026 AcadeGrade · Personal academic planning, not an official university record · Privacy notice".
- **No Firestore, no auth, no client JS** in the shell itself, so it renders when services are down; the SVG mark is inline so it needs no image request. Theme follows the root `next-themes` class.

## 9. SEO

- **Robots:** noindex, nofollow, nocache (kept via `shareMetadataFor`). Never in the sitemap.
- **Titles and descriptions:** carried from `shareMetadataFor`: live, non-PIN links "{Name}'s unofficial academic record | AcadeGrade"; PIN, expired and unknown links the generic "Shared transcript | AcadeGrade" with no name.
- **Open Graph / Twitter:** the generic slip image (§6) for every case.

## 10. Accessibility

- One h1 per state (the paper's h1 on ready; the stub's h1 otherwise). Semesters are h2s.
- Tables: real `<table>` with `<caption>`, `<th scope="col">`, numeric columns right-aligned; on phones the two-line rows keep the header association with `data-label` text for screen readers.
- The notice band is `role="note"`; the ArcMeter has a text alternative; the stripe and perforations are `aria-hidden`.
- PIN field: labelled, described by the hint and the error, `aria-invalid` on error; disabled state explained in text.
- Contrast: examiner red on paper about 5.3:1; ink on gold about 9:1; paper text on ink about 16:1.
- Buttons 44px; "Print" padded to 44px.
