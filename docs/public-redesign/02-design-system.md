# 02 · Design system for the public site

The components and rules every public page is built from. Brand reasoning is in `01-brand-and-art-direction.md`. When Phase A ships, this file becomes the new `acadegrade-web/DESIGN.md` for the public surface (the old `DESIGN.md` stays for the student and admin pages until they are migrated).

## 1. Where it lives in code

- **Tokens:** `app/(public)/public.css`. Public-only tokens prefixed `--pg-`, imported by the public layout, so student and admin pages are untouched.
- **Shared layout:** a new `app/(public)/layout.tsx` (there is none today). It renders `PublicHeader`, the page, then `PublicFooter`. Auth routes use their own shell; system routes use the minimal shell (see `04-site-map-navigation-seo.md`).
- **Components:** `components/public/`, one folder per component with its test (`Slip`, `ArcMeter`, `Stripe`, `PublicHeader`, `MenuSheet`, `PublicFooter`, `Button`, `TextLink`, `Accordion`, `Field`, `GradeKeys`, `ReplyCard`, `Letter`, `TicketStub`, `UiFrame`, `Credit`, `CookieStub`, `SectionHeading`, `Figure`).
- **Retire after migration:** `components/marketing/*` (except the logic worth keeping: the calculator handoff and legal rendering), `LandingMotion`, `PublicTextMotion`, `components/layout/Navbar.tsx`, `PublicHeader` (old) and `PublicShell`, plus `.public-atmosphere` and the two background PNGs.

## 2. Frame and grid

- **Grid:** 12 columns, gutter 24px (16px on phones). One pair of outer margins, `--pg-margin`, used by the header, every section and the footer. The content column is limited to 1360px; fields of colour and full-bleed photographs run edge to edge.
- **Section rhythm:** `--pg-gap-section` between sections (72px phone to 160px desktop), always clearly larger than any gap inside a section.
- **Text edges:** at most three left edges per page: the margin, column 7, and the slip's inner padding.
- **Fullness:** no stretch taller than half a screen holds nothing. When text takes the left half, the right half holds its picture, slip or figure.
- **Phone:** each section is recomposed for the phone, not squeezed. The object (slip, sheet, card) goes full width inside the margins and keeps its shaped edge.

## 3. Stationery edges (the only ornaments)

All edges are filled shapes. There are no outlines, no hairlines and no shadows at rest.

| Edge | How | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| **Perforation** | A row of round dots: `border-top: var(--pg-perf) dotted var(--pg-ground)` (renders round dots in Chromium at 4px and up; test Safari, with an SVG pattern fallback) | Between a slip and its tear-off stub; above slip totals |
| **Zigzag tear** | `clip-path: polygon(...)` generated by a helper (`zigzag(n, depth)`), depth 10px, teeth 20px | Bottom of receipt/itinerary slips |
| **Scallop** | Inline SVG path, or `mask` with repeating radial shapes (a mask, not a visible gradient) | Footer top edge, letter bottom edge |
| **Notch** | `clip-path` with two half-circle cut-outs (ticket) | Ticket stubs (testimonials, share-link stubs) |
| **Stripe band** | Five flat blocks `--pg-step-1…5`, equal widths (flex), 56px tall (40px phone) | Top of the hero slip; 8px above the footer; inside the primary button on hover |

## 4. Components

Each component lists its anatomy, sizes (mapped to the five type steps), states and behaviour. All interactive targets are at least 44×44px.

### 4.1 PublicHeader

- **Anatomy:** a plain bar of height 72px (60px on phones), on the page's outer margins.
  - Left: the AcadeGrade mark (SVG, 32px) and the wordmark "AcadeGrade" in one ink, no gradient.
  - Centre-left: four links: **Features**, **Free tools**, **Android app**, **About**.
  - Right: **Sign in** (text link) and **Get started** (primary button, small).
  - Signed in: the right side becomes **Open dashboard**.
- **Behaviour:**
  - Sticky. Transparent over the ground at the top; once scrolled past 8px it takes a solid `--pg-ground` fill. No blur and no shadow; a tone step from the ground does the separating.
  - Hides while scrolling down and returns on scroll up (transform only), never on pages shorter than two screens.
  - Current page: the link gets a 3px ink underline set 6px below the text (a filled bar, not a hairline) and `aria-current="page"`.
- **Phone:** the mark, the wordmark and a **Menu** button (text label plus icon).
- **Removed:** the theme switch moves to the footer.

### 4.2 MenuSheet (phone navigation)

- **Element:** a native `<dialog>` opened by the Menu button. It is a full-height paper slip sliding from the top, with a stripe band at its top.
  - Contents: the same four links at `--pg-head`, then **Calculator**, **Support**, **Sign in**, and **Get started** as a full-width button.
  - Closing: Esc, the Close button and the backdrop all close it. Focus is trapped while open and returns to Menu on close.
- **Motion:** slides down 24px with opacity in 320ms with `--pg-ease-paper`. Under reduced motion it fades only.

### 4.3 PublicFooter: "the back of the card"

- **The card:** a paper panel spanning columns 2–11 on the ground, with its top edge scalloped (12 scallops desktop, 6 phone) and an 8px stripe band above it.
- **Left half:**
  - A sign-off at `--pg-head`: "Good luck this semester."
  - Two lines at body: "AcadeGrade is made in Enugu for Nigerian university students. Your record stays yours."
  - The appearance control: a three-way segmented control, System / Light / Dark.
- **Right half:** link groups set like an address block.
  - **Product:** Features, Result scanner, AcadeMind, Timetable, Android app.
  - **Free tools:** CGPA calculator, Degree class checker, Required GPA, Carry-over impact, Weighted average.
  - **Help and legal:** Support, Privacy notice, Terms of use, Delete your account, Cookie choices.
  - **Stamp:** the support email as a link, and a small greyscale "stamp" photograph (the UNN gate, 3:4, 88px wide, 6px paper margin) in the top-right corner.
- **Imprint line** (small, centred, along the bottom): "© 2026 AcadeGrade · Personal academic planning, not an official university record · Photographs: …credits…".
- **Phone:** the panel runs full width, the groups stack, and the stamp sits top-right at 64px.

### 4.4 Buttons and links (one shape, decided once)

- **Primary button:** square corners (radius 0), filled `--pg-ink`, text `--pg-paper` at body weight 500. Height 52px (44px for `size="sm"`), padding 0 24px.
  - Hover: a 4px stripe band rises from the bottom edge inside the button (transform, 160ms).
  - Press: translateY(1px).
  - Focus: a 3px `--pg-gold` outline offset 3px.
  - Disabled: `--pg-ink-soft` fill, `aria-disabled`.
  - Busy: the label stays, a small spinner appears at the right, and the width stays fixed.
- **On ink or violet fields:** the same button filled `--pg-paper` with `--pg-ink` text.
- **Text link:** ink, with a real text underline (`text-decoration-thickness: 2px; text-underline-offset: 4px`), not a border or rule. Hover thickens to 3px. Visited is unchanged.
- **One primary action per screen.** Secondary actions are text links, never a second outlined button.

### 4.5 Slip: the core object

The base for every stationery piece. A paper panel with square corners and believable proportions. Every variant uses the same anatomy:

1. A stripe band (56px; 40px on phones), or a plain header band for quieter pages.
2. A header block, centred like a printed slip:
   - the AcadeGrade mark (24px);
   - "Statement of result" or the slip's own title, at lead;
   - the meta line at small: example student, level, programme, session and semester.
3. A body table (a real `<table>`, so 1px row lines are allowed inside it):
   - columns: Code, Course title, Units, CA, Exam, Total, Grade, Point;
   - at body size with tabular figures;
   - on phones, Title wraps under Code, and CA and Exam fold into Total.
4. A perforation.
5. A foot bar: GPA, CGPA and class in an ink bar with paper text. The highlighted figure sits on gold with ink text.

**Variants:**

- `result` (hero, calculator);
- `receipt` (how it works; zigzag bottom);
- `letter` (about; scalloped bottom plus a tear-off strip);
- `stub` (ticket notch; testimonials, share links);
- `sheet` (feature stacks, full-width coloured);
- `reply` (CTA card, rotated 2°, cropped by the section edge).

### 4.6 ArcMeter

- **Shape:** the logo's arc, drawn in SVG as five flat stroke segments (`--pg-step-1…5`), not a gradient. Three-quarter arc from 0.00 to 5.00, with a gold dot at the current value.
- **Ticks:** 1.50, 2.40, 3.50 and 4.50, labelled with their class names at small size on desktop and as abbreviations (Pass, 3rd, 2:2, 2:1, 1st) on phones.
- **Accessibility:** `role="img"` with a label such as "CGPA 4.12 of 5.00, Second Class Upper".
- **Motion:** the dot travels along the arc in 320ms on value change (reduced motion: it jumps).

### 4.7 GradeKeys

- **Shape:** a row of six square keys, A to F, at least 44px on phones. The selected key is ink-filled; F, when selected, uses `--pg-mark`.
- **Behaviour:** a radio group (`role="radiogroup"`), with arrow keys moving the choice.

### 4.8 Fields (calculator, tools, auth)

- **Shape:** square, filled `--pg-paper-2`, with a 1px `--pg-ink-soft` bottom border (form fields may have lines). Height 52px.
  - The label always sits above the field at small size, weight 500.
  - Hints and limits sit below and are linked with `aria-describedby`.
- **Error:** the bottom border turns `--pg-mark` 2px, the message appears below with a text icon, and focus moves to the first error on submit.
- **Number fields:** `inputmode="decimal"`, with steppers only where there are clear integer steps (units).

### 4.9 Accordion

- **Element:** native `<details>`/`<summary>`, with no lines between rows, only 24–28px of space.
  - The summary is at lead size with a chevron drawn from two 2px strokes, rotating 180° in 160ms.
  - The answer is at body size in full ink.
- **FAQ data:** comes from one typed list per page and also feeds the FAQPage JSON-LD.

### 4.10 UiFrame (real interface fragments)

- **Root:** carries `data-ui`, which lets the product's own borders and controls show inside it.
- **Crop and scale:** a crop of a real screen, built from the real student components with sample data, or a 2x screenshot. Scaled to about 70–100%, square corners, on paper or on a field colour.
  - It carries a caption at small size saying what it shows ("Semester view on Android").
- **Banned:** glossy device mockups, tilted 3D phones and fake grey-bar dashboards. A flat ink phone outline (no shine) is allowed on the Android page only.

### 4.11 CookieStub (replaces the cookie panel)

- **Desktop:** a ticket stub in the bottom-right corner, 360px wide, with a notched left edge.
  - Contents: one sentence at small size ("We use necessary storage to keep you signed in. With your OK, Google AdSense may use cookies to show ads.").
  - Buttons: **Allow ads & analytics** and **Necessary only** with equal weight (the label the Privacy notice already quotes) (both paper-on-ink, NDPA-fair), plus a **Choices** text link.
- **Phone:** a bottom bar at most 132px tall (two lines plus a button row) that never covers the primary action. The hero adds bottom padding equal to the stub's height while it is open.
- **Behaviour:** the same consent logic and storage as today. It is called "Privacy notice" (not "Privacy Policy"), and the footer's "Cookie choices" reopens it.

### 4.12 SectionHeading

An `h2` at head size, two lines at most, followed by one sentence at lead size placed 24–40px below. There is never an eyebrow above it.

### 4.13 Figure and Credit

- **Figure:** a `<figure>` with `next/image` (AVIF/WebP, explicit sizes), with lazy loading below the fold and `priority` only for the first-screen photograph.
- **Caption:** at small size, saying what the picture is.
- **Credit:** credits are collected into the footer imprint line by a small registry (`lib/public/credits.ts`), so every found picture's credit appears automatically.

### 4.14 ProofStub and CountLine (owner-requested proof)

- **ProofStub:**
  - A ticket stub with a quote at lead size (25 words at most), the first name plus level, department and university at small size, and a notch.
  - Shown in groups of three, staggered.
  - Hidden in production unless three or more quotes have been approved (see `01` §8).
- **CountLine:**
  - One sentence with real figures from `admin_metrics/latest`: "Used by 1,240 students across 38 Nigerian universities".
  - Shown only above the admin threshold; otherwise it shows the fallback fact.

### 4.15 Components added by the sign-in and system page plans

- **`ChoiceKeys`:** a generic square-key single-choice group. `GradeKeys` is built on it, and register uses it for age, entry level and the record-setup choice.
- **`Stepper`:** minus/value/plus for integers (units, course duration, spill-over semesters), replacing sliders.
- **`StepStripe`:** the five-block brand stripe used as a progress meter on register and reset password. The gold block fills only when the account is created.
- **`CodeField`:** the 6-digit verification code. It accepts a pasted line, uses tabular figures, checks itself at the sixth digit, and states its limits ("expires in 5 minutes").
- **`FilledSoFar`:** a read-only stub summarising earlier register steps.
- **`ChoiceRow`:** a full-width selectable row (record setup: from scratch or complete record).
- **`AuthShell`:** with `GoogleButton`, `PasswordField` (show/hide), `PerforationDivider` and `AuthMessage`; copy lives in `lib/public/auth-copy.ts`. The Google button is the one permitted second button: Google's branding requires its own button with the coloured G, so it sits on the paper-row fill while ink stays the only primary fill.
- **`MinimalShell`:** used by the share pages, maintenance, offline, iPhone and 404. It must render without Firestore.
- **`RecordPaper`:** the shared transcript as an A4 paper record, with print styles. Dark bands become ruled rows in print.
- **`VisitorStub`:** a quiet "What is AcadeGrade?" tear-off on share pages.

## 5. States every page must design

- **Data states:** loading (skeleton slips with the same shape, no shimmer gradient), empty, error (a slip with the message and a retry link), offline (a plain line), maintenance (the system page).
- **Feature switches:**
  - `share_code` off → the share-link page shows "Course sharing is paused";
  - signups paused → register shows its notice;
  - `timetable` off → the timetable feature page still explains the feature but the "Open timetable" action goes to sign-in.
- **Theme:** light and dark screenshots are part of each page's gate.

## 6. Accessibility

- **Headings:** one `h1` per page and a heading on every section, in landmark order: header, main, footer.
- **Navigation:** a skip link, "Skip to content", appears as the first focusable element.
- **Focus:** visible everywhere (3px gold outline; violet on gold fields).
- **Images:** alt text describes the picture; decorative stripes and edges are `aria-hidden`.
- **Live regions:** the hero slip announces its new GPA politely (`aria-live="polite"`, debounced 500ms).
- **Contrast:** every pair in `01` §4 passes AA, and text never sits on a photograph without a neutral scrim.
- **Sizes:** text is never below 14px; running text is 17px or more.

## 7. Performance budget (phones on mobile data)

- **Targets:** LCP under 2.0s on a mid Android over 4G (Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 for performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO); CLS under 0.05; INP under 200ms.
- **Weight:**
  - Public-page JavaScript under 90 KB gzipped beyond the framework. Marketing sections are server components; only the live slip, calculators, menu, cookie stub and theme control are client islands.
  - First-screen photograph under 180 KB (AVIF); total first-load image weight under 400 KB. No CSS background PNGs.
  - Fonts: Hanken Grotesk, two weights, subset Latin, about 40 KB.
- **Caching:** fix `next.config.ts`, which currently sends `Cache-Control: no-cache, no-store` for every path. Static assets and images must be cacheable (`immutable` for hashed assets); only HTML and APIs stay no-store where needed.
- **Dead code to drop:** `three` and the unused 3D components, the unused `mobile-app-images` files, and the background PNGs.
- **Data saver:** honour the existing data-saver flag and `navigator.connection.saveData`: no hero crossfade, no video autoplay, smaller image widths.

## 8. Quality gate for every page (Definition of Done)

1. `node ~/.claude/skills/design-taste-frontend/scripts/lint.mjs` on changed files: clean.
2. `inspect.mjs` on the production build (`next build && next start`) at 1440, 768 and 390: **0 FAIL**, with every WARN defended in the page plan.
3. `impeccable detect --json` on changed files: clean, or each finding defended.
4. Every screenshot opened and critiqued (tasteskill `critique.md`), with the top three problems fixed. Light and dark.
5. Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 in all four categories; keyboard-only walkthrough; a screen-reader spot check of the h1, the live slip and forms.
6. Unit tests for each component and each page's data (sample data adds up; FAQ JSON-LD matches the visible FAQ; links resolve).
7. Every link that worked before still works, and old URLs redirect where they moved.
